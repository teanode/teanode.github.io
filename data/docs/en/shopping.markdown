Shopping keeps named lists, such as Groceries or Hardware, that you share with
a [circle](/doc/circles) or a person. At the store everybody the list reaches
sees what goes into the cart as it happens, and a receipt checks off what it
bought. Every item remembers what it cost at each store, finds its own
picture, and your phone reminds you what your lists need when you arrive at a
store.

## Lists and items

An item has a description, details for whoever shops ("Fuji preferred"), how
many and of what, the store to get it from or any store, an aisle, a picture
and its latest price. It is **needed**, **in the cart**, or **not needed**. A
bought item stays on its list with its picture and prices, so a list is used
week after week: **Need again** puts it back. Adding something already on
the list needs the one that is there again instead of adding a second.

## Shopping a store

At the store you shop the store, not a list. The store's screen gathers what
every list you reach needs there, and what any store will do, in aisle order,
each item saying which list it is on.

There is no Start button. The first item you put in the cart starts your
trip and tells the others the list reaches that you are shopping. Each
check shows on their screens at once. **Done** buys what is in your cart.

## Receipts

Every receipt TeaNode keeps, whether photographed on a list, sorted from your
mail or sent to your agent, is matched against your lists. A sure match checks
the item off and records the price at that store; an unsure one waits for you
to apply or dismiss. **Undo** puts back exactly what a match changed.

## Sharing

Share a list with one person in your circle or the whole circle. Everybody it
reaches can add, change and check off items; only you share it further or
delete it. Shared lists show on the Sharing page with everything else you
share. Leaving the circle ends the shares it made possible.

## Pictures and reminders

A new item looks for its picture on the web: your agent searches, picks the
product's own page and keeps its picture only if it really shows the item.
Pictures are served by your server, so your phone never loads anything from
the shop's site. You can upload your own instead.

When your phone arrives at a store, with [Location](/doc/location) on, it tells
you what your lists need there. Mute a list or a store if you would rather
not hear about it.

## Your agent

Tell your agent "add eggs and rice to the groceries", or paste or photograph
a list, and the items go on it. It reads and changes the lists you reach, like
you do, and asks first before it deletes, removes items or shares.

On the command line it is `teanode shopping`; `teanode shopping --help` lists
every command.
